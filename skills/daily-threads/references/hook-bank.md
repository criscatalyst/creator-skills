# Hook Bank

The hook is the only line that decides whether the post is read. Pick an archetype on purpose. Never invent a hook from scratch when a proven shape fits the material.

## 10 hook archetypes (HookForge)

| # | Archetype | When to use | Base template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Age / Experience Lead** | Specific experience to lean on | "I'm [age]. After [X yrs] in [field], here's what no one teaches you:" |
| 2 | **Defining Moment** | A breakthrough/collapse story | "[Date]: everything fell apart. Here's what I rebuilt." |
| 3 | **Achievement Highlight** | Real, verifiable numbers | "[Big #] in [time]. The N moves that did it." |
| 4 | **Hard Truth / Contrarian** | An unpopular but defensible take | "[Common belief] is riskier than [thing]. Here's the math:" |
| 5 | **List Preview** | N concrete tips | "N [things] I'd tattoo on my [age]-yr-old self 👇" |
| 6 | **Audience Call-Out** | Filter the right readers | "If you're [avatar] and still [pain], read this." |
| 7 | **Empathy / Pain-Point** | Build rapport before the fix | "It's brutal trying to [common struggle]." |
| 8 | **How-To Promise** | Immediate value sell | "How to [outcome] without [obstacle]:" |
| 9 | **Surprising Fact** | A stat or counter-intuitive data | "[Source] found [stat]. Here's how to use it:" |
| 10 | **Vivid Scene** | Immersive storytelling | "[Sensory detail]. I realized [truth]. Now:" |

**Killer combo:** Age + Contrarian + List → max scroll-stop. e.g. "I'm 27. 6 years building agency systems. 5 lessons every solo founder ignores:"

## Hook rules

- "I" or "you" in the hook itself when possible.
- ≥ 2 power verbs in the hook (bank below).
- For a thread, the hook must promise a payoff the rest delivers. No bait-and-switch.
- For VIEWS/GROWTH: pick a broad, mass-appeal hook (1, 4, 5, 7).
- For SALES / LEADS: pick a specific, qualifying hook (3, 6, 8) — it filters for the right buyer.
- Always offer the user 2-3 hook options for the lead post when there's room to choose.

## Thread hook patterns (for Template 0)

- `"I [did X]. Not with [common tool]. With [unexpected thing]. Here's how 🧵"`
- `"[Bold claim about the result]. Here's my entire workflow 🧵"`
- `"[Pain everyone has]. So I built [solution]. Here's how 🧵"`
- `"[Surprising number/fact]. Here's why 🧵"`
