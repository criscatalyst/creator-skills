# Hook engine

The hook is the video. If the first second doesn't stop the thumb, nothing after it exists.

## A hook has three layers

| Layer | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **First frame** | Stop the thumb before a word is heard (most people scroll muted) | Motion, a face mid-sentence, or the proof itself (the result, the number, the before/after). Never a title card or a slow intro. |
| **On-screen text** | Make the hook readable in 1 second with sound off | ≤ 6 words. Adds to the spoken line instead of repeating it word for word. |
| **Spoken line** | Open a loop the viewer needs closed | ≤ 12 words. Starts on the strongest word, not on "So" or "Hey". |

## Mechanics

Write 10 hooks across **at least 6** of these. Each mechanic is a different reason to stay, which is what keeps scripts from sounding the same.

| # | Mechanic | Shape | Example (with numbers = measured outlier, Sept 2026 · views · × the account's median; without = illustration) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Callout** | You're doing X wrong / wasting Y | "This is how most of you guys use ChatGPT…" |
| 2 | **Paid vs free** | Price of the thing they pay for vs a free alternative | "$24,000/year terminal vs. a free model on GitHub." (114k · 47×) |
| 3 | **Proof first** | The result is on screen before the claim | Show the finished output, then "Claude made this in one prompt." |
| 4 | **Stakes / loss** | What they're losing right now, as a number | "You're burning a week of AI credits in two hours." |
| 5 | **Enemy / killed** | A tool or trend ends a job, a cost or a habit | "Claude just killed web designers." (600k · 15×) |
| 6 | **Counted promise** | Exact number of items + the outcome | "Five power words for ChatGPT." (208k · 108×) · "Here are 10 secret codes for ChatGPT." (227k · 66×) |
| 7 | **Test / experiment** | A real trial with an uncertain result | "I just gave GPT-6 Astra $10,000 to trade stocks for me." (213k · 5×) |
| 8 | **Chore handed off** | AI does a task everyone hates, end to end | "You don't need to book a Tatkal ticket now, AI will do it for you." (1.1M · 38×) |
| 9 | **Secret / nobody tells you** | Hidden cause behind a common pain | "Nobody tells you why your limits run out." |
| 10 | **Deictic "this"** | Points at something on screen and withholds what it does | "This file makes Claude write like you." (the file is on screen) |
| 11 | **Contrarian** | Against what the niche repeats | "Stop learning prompt engineering." |
| 12 | **Identity callout** | Names exactly who it's for | "If you post every day and hate editing, watch this." |

The examples age fast. **Always prefer the creator's fresh sources** (hook bank, last month's outlier transcripts) over this table, and adapt a mechanic, never copy a line.

## Power words

Every hook carries at least one. A power word is **concrete stakes**, not hype:
- **Loss and threat:** stop, delete, killed, wasting, burning, losing, mistake, wrong, never
- **Money:** free, a real price ($24,000/year, $20/month), paying, cost
- **Secrecy:** secret, nobody, hidden, leaked, most people
- **Speed and ease:** a real duration (10 seconds, one prompt, one file), without, automatically
- **Proof:** a real number, a named tool, a named result

Hype adjectives ("insane", "crazy", "mind-blowing", "game-changer") are not power words: at most one per script, never as the only one in the hook.

## Pain inside the hook

Name the pain the viewer already feels, in their words ("you're paying for ElevenLabs every month", "your captions sound like ChatGPT"). If the hook only describes the video ("in this video I'll show you…"), it's a description, not a hook.

## The gap

The hook promises and withholds. If the viewer can guess the answer from the hook alone, it's dead: rewrite so the *what* or the *how* stays hidden until the payoff.

## Scoring (1-5 each, /25)

| Criterion | 5 = |
|---|---|
| **Stop** | The target viewer stops in under a second, even muted |
| **Specific** | Names a tool, number, price, duration or result |
| **Gap** | The payoff can't be guessed from the hook |
| **Pain / stakes** | The viewer's own pain or loss is in it |
| **Ownable** | True for this creator, backed by something they can show |

Drop anything under 18. **Ownable < 3 is disqualifying**: a hook the creator can't prove is a lie, however well it scores elsewhere.

## Dead openers (never)

"In today's video", "Hey guys", "Did you know", "Have you ever", "This is your sign", "Let me show you", "Here's the thing", "X changed everything", "The truth about X", "You won't believe", questions answered by yes/no, and any hook that states the lesson.
